# IPL champions by season (2008-2025)

2008 Rajasthan Royals (beat Chennai Super Kings). 2009 Deccan Chargers (beat
RCB, season in South Africa). 2010 Chennai Super Kings. 2011 Chennai Super
Kings (first back-to-back). 2012 Kolkata Knight Riders (beat CSK). 2013 Mumbai
Indians. 2014 Kolkata Knight Riders. 2015 Mumbai Indians. 2016 Sunrisers
Hyderabad (beat RCB). 2017 Mumbai Indians. 2018 Chennai Super Kings (title on
return from suspension). 2019 Mumbai Indians (beat CSK by 1 run, closest
final). 2020 Mumbai Indians (UAE season, fifth title). 2021 Chennai Super
Kings (fourth title). 2022 Gujarat Titans (champions in debut season). 2023
Chennai Super Kings (fifth title, equalling MI). 2024 Kolkata Knight Riders
(third title). 2025 Royal Challengers Bengaluru (first title, beating Punjab
Kings by 6 runs). Most titles: Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings with five
each. Teams yet to win through 2025 include Punjab Kings and Delhi Capitals.
