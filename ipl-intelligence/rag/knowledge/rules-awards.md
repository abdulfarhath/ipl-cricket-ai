# IPL season awards explained

Orange Cap: worn (literally, in matches) and finally awarded to the season's
leading run scorer; the lead can change hands during the tournament. Purple
Cap: the season's leading wicket taker, same rolling mechanism. Most Valuable
Player: awarded on a points formula rewarding match-impact acts — runs,
boundary hitting, wickets, dot balls, catches — rather than raw totals.
Emerging Player of the Season: best young (typically under-23, criteria have
varied) player. Fair Play Award: judged on umpire assessments of teams'
conduct. Per match, a Player of the Match is named; in this assistant's
database, Player-of-the-Match awards are recorded for every match and
career leaders can be computed. Orange and Purple Cap standings in this
assistant are computed live from ball-by-ball data, which matches the official
results.
