# IPL rule and format changes timeline, 2008-2025

2008: launch with eight teams, double round-robin and semi-finals; the player
auction model debuts. 2009: season moved to South Africa (Indian elections);
strategic timeouts introduced. 2010: first season broadcast live on YouTube.
2011: expansion to ten teams; the Page playoff system (Qualifier 1, Eliminator,
Qualifier 2) replaces semi-finals and remains today. 2012-2013: back to nine
then eight teams as franchises exited; spot-fixing scandal of 2013 led to
tighter anti-corruption oversight. 2014: leg-side wide interpretation
tightened; part of the season hosted in the UAE (elections). 2016-2017: CSK
and RR suspended; Gujarat Lions and Rising Pune Supergiant fill in. 2018:
both return; DRS (player reviews) used in the IPL for the first time. 2019:
one review per side per innings (later expanded). 2020: full season in the
UAE (COVID-19); third umpire given the front-foot no-ball call on every
delivery. 2021: season suspended mid-way by COVID and completed in the UAE.
2022: permanent expansion to ten teams (Gujarat Titans, Lucknow Super Giants);
two groups league format for one season; two DRS reviews per innings. 2023:
Impact Player substitute rule; captains may name their XI after the toss;
wides and no-balls reviewable under DRS; WPL (Women's Premier League)
launches. 2024: two bouncers per over permitted (adopted from BCCI domestic
rules); Smart Replay System with direct ball-tracking access for the TV
umpire; over-rate field penalties enforced in-innings. 2025: Royal Challengers
Bengaluru win their first title; mega-auction cycle rules applied with
expanded purse and Right-to-Match provisions; Impact Player confirmed to
continue through the cycle. Data cutoff of this assistant: 31 December 2025.
