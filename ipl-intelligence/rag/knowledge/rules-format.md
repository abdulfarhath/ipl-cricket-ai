# IPL tournament format and key rules

The IPL is a Twenty20 competition: each side bats a maximum of 20 overs. The
league phase is a round-robin (double round-robin in most seasons; group-based
in some 10-team seasons), followed by playoffs. Since 2011 the playoffs use the
Page format: Qualifier 1 between the top two league teams (winner goes straight
to the final), an Eliminator between third and fourth, and Qualifier 2 between
the Qualifier 1 loser and the Eliminator winner. Wins are worth two points, a
no-result one point; ties on points are broken by net run rate. Tied matches are
decided by a Super Over. Distinctive IPL rules over the years include the
strategic timeout (a short mid-innings break), a cap on overseas players (four
in the playing XI), and, from the 2023 season, the Impact Player rule allowing a
team to substitute one player from a named list during the match. Season-long
individual awards include the Orange Cap for most runs and the Purple Cap for
most wickets.
