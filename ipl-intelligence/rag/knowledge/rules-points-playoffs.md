# IPL points system, net run rate and playoff structure

League stage: every win earns 2 points, a no-result 1 point, a loss 0. Teams
level on points are separated by Net Run Rate (NRR). NRR = (total runs scored ÷
total overs faced) − (total runs conceded ÷ total overs bowled), computed
across the league stage; when a side is bowled out, the full quota of 20 overs
is used in the calculation rather than the overs actually faced. Ties in a
match itself are broken by a Super Over, so a tied game still produces a
2-point winner.

Playoffs (since 2011, the "Page playoff" format): the top four league teams
qualify. Qualifier 1 pits 1st vs 2nd — the winner goes directly to the final.
The Eliminator pits 3rd vs 4th — the loser is out. Qualifier 2 pits the loser
of Qualifier 1 against the winner of the Eliminator, and its winner takes the
second final spot. This rewards finishing top-two with a second chance. Before
2011 the IPL used conventional semi-finals. The final is a single match; the
winner is champion. Season-long individual honours include the Orange Cap
(most runs), Purple Cap (most wickets), Most Valuable Player (by a points
formula), and the Emerging Player award for young players.
