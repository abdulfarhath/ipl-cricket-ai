# Origins and formation of the IPL

The Indian Premier League (IPL) was founded by the Board of Control for Cricket
in India (BCCI) in 2007, with the first season played in April-June 2008. It was
established as a franchise-based Twenty20 competition, drawing on the model of
professional sports leagues: city-based teams owned by private investors, a
player auction, and a broadcast-first commercial design. Lalit Modi was the
league's chief architect and first commissioner. The creation of the IPL
followed the surge of interest in Twenty20 cricket after India won the inaugural
ICC World Twenty20 in 2007, and it competed directly with the unsanctioned
Indian Cricket League (ICL), which folded soon after. The first match was played
on 18 April 2008, and Rajasthan Royals, led by Shane Warne, won the inaugural
title, defeating Chennai Super Kings in the final. The league began with eight
franchises: Chennai Super Kings, Deccan Chargers, Delhi Daredevils, Kings XI
Punjab, Kolkata Knight Riders, Mumbai Indians, Rajasthan Royals, and Royal
Challengers Bangalore.
