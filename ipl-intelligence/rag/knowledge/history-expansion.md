# Franchise changes and league expansion

The IPL began in 2008 with eight teams. Pune Warriors India and Kochi Tuskers
Kerala joined in 2011, briefly making it ten; Kochi lasted one season and Pune
withdrew after 2013. Deccan Chargers, champions in 2009, were replaced by
Sunrisers Hyderabad from 2013. Between 2016 and 2017, Chennai Super Kings and
Rajasthan Royals served suspensions, with Gujarat Lions and Rising Pune
Supergiant filling in; both suspended teams returned in 2018, and CSK won the
title that year. In 2022 the league expanded permanently to ten teams with
Gujarat Titans (champions in their debut season) and Lucknow Super Giants.
Several franchises rebranded over time: Delhi Daredevils became Delhi Capitals
(2019), Kings XI Punjab became Punjab Kings (2021), and Royal Challengers
Bangalore restyled as Royal Challengers Bengaluru, who won their first title in
2025. The 2009 season was hosted in South Africa due to Indian general
elections, and the 2020 season was played in the UAE due to the COVID-19
pandemic.
