# IPL auction, squad and transfer rules

IPL squads are built primarily through a player auction. A mega auction
(roughly every three years) rebuilds squads almost from scratch, while smaller
annual mini auctions fill gaps. Before a mega auction, teams may retain a
limited number of players (retention slots and costs are set per cycle) and may
use Right to Match (RTM) cards at the auction to buy back a former player by
matching the winning bid. Each franchise operates under a salary purse (a
spending cap set by the BCCI for each cycle) covering its full squad, typically
around 18-25 players with a cap on overseas players (usually eight in the
squad, four in a playing XI). Players register with a base price; bidding
starts there and the highest bidder wins. Unsold players can return in
accelerated rounds. Between seasons there are trade windows in which
franchises may trade players (with player consent and within purse rules),
either as straight cash deals or player swaps. Replacement signings are allowed
during a season only for ruled-out players, from the pool of registered
unsold players. Uncapped Indian players have separate base-price rules, and a
notable recent provision restored uncapped status for long-retired
internationals. The auction system is why player "transfers" in the IPL mostly
happen as auction moves rather than football-style transfers.
